# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / 行為準則

🌐 English · 繁體中文

## Our pledge / 我們的承諾

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

我們承諾讓社群參與成為一個對所有人都友善、無騷擾的體驗，不論年齡、體型、
可見或不可見的身心障礙、族裔、性別、經驗程度、教育、社經地位、國籍、
外貌、種族、宗教或性傾向。

## Our standards / 行為標準

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment / 正面行為範例:

- Being kind and patient, especially with beginners. / 友善、有耐心，尤其對新手。
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback. / 給予並虛心接受建設性回饋。
- Focusing on what is best for the community. / 以社群整體利益為先。

Unacceptable behavior / 不可接受的行為:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments. / 騷擾、侮辱或貶低言論。
- Publishing others' private information without permission. / 未經同意公開他人隱私。

## Enforcement / 執行

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers
via a GitHub issue or direct contact. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

如遇不可接受的行為，可透過 GitHub issue 或直接聯繫維護者回報。所有申訴都會
被即時且公正地審視與處理。
